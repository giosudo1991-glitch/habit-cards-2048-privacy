Privacy Policy for Habit drop & merge

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Overview

Habit drop & merge ("the app") is a puzzle game. This policy explains what information is used when you play the app.

Information we collect

We do not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, or any other personal data. We do not directly collect or store personal information on our own servers.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress, scores, stars, settings, and in-game items are saved locally on your device only. This data is not sent to us. If you uninstall the app, this data is deleted.

Advertising

The app displays ads provided by Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service. AdMob may collect and use certain information from your device in order to show ads and measure their performance, including your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and operating system information, and information about how you interact with ads. This may include personalized ads, depending on your device settings and your consent choices where required by law.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (on Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads). For more information about how Google uses data, see: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
