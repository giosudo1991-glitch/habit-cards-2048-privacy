Privacy Policy for Habit drop & merge

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Overview

Habit drop & merge ("the app") is a puzzle game. We respect your privacy. This policy explains what information the app uses.

Information we collect

The app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. We do not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, or any other personal data.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your game progress, scores, stars, settings, and in-game items locally on your device only. This data is not sent to us or to any third party. If you uninstall the app, this data is deleted.

Third-party services

The app does not use third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
